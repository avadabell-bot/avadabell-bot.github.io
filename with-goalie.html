With a Goalkeeper Grade!

Standard Rules of Field Hockey Apply except the following modifications

All Teams must supply their own umpire.

  • Games are played on half a turf with boards acting as a sideline.
  • Seven players per team may be on the turf at any time, Including the Goalkeeper.
  • Substitutions can happen any time except during penalty corners.
  • Goalkeepers can use their whole body inside the circle and use only their stick outside the circle.
  • The ball can be raised to any height when dribbling or passing so long as it remains below the knee and under control.
  • Any shot (sweep, flick, hit, deflection) at goal may be raised above backboard height if it is controlled and on target.
  • Auto passes are permitted on restarts.
  • Penalty corners are awarded for infringements from defenders inside the circle.
  • No penalty strokes are to be awarded.
  • Goals are scored when the ball is played by an attacker inside the circle and crosses fully over the line into the goal.

Penalty Corners 'PC'

Defending a PC
  • 3 defenders behind the goal line, with the goalkeeper OR 4 defenders in goal if no goalkeeper
  • Remaining defenders must be in the opposite goal circle
  • When the ball has left the baseline after the umpires whistle, the defenders may defend the attacking play.
  • Note: It is highly recommended to wear safety equipment especially masks. Gloves and knee pads can also be used.
Attacking a Penalty Corner
  • An attacker places the ball out from the base line, 12 m from centre of the goal.
  • The remaining attackers any outside the circle.
  • Upon the whistle of the umpire, the attacker drags or hits the ball across the circle.
  • The ball must leave the circle before a goal can be scored in a PC.
  • A hit must cross the line below the backboard. A lifted ball is allowed if it is a flick or deflection.
  • If the first hit/sweep on a PC causes the ball to travel above the backboard height it becomes a free hit for raised ball to defenders.
  • Shots following the first shot can be raised as long as it is controlled and on target.
A Penalty Corner is over when any of the following happens:
  • A goal is scored,
  • The ball travels 5 m outside the circle,
  • Free hit or 16 is given to the defensive team,
  • A long corner is awarded for the attacking team.
